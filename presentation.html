What already exists

IMD provides Tropical Weather Outlooks, national/hourly bulletins, cyclone tracks, wind warnings, storm-surge warnings, interactive tracks and preliminary reports.

What our project adds

A unified research dashboard for multi-source satellite-image analysis, pattern classification, historical similarity, environmental feature analysis, explainability and model validation.

Important boundary

This prototype does not replace IMD and does not issue official warnings. The production version should compare its outputs against authoritative observations and forecasts.
